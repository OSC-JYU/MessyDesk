MD-UVDoc Service

The MD-UVDoc service dewarps photographed document images, removing the perspective and page-curl distortion typical of phone-camera captures so that downstream OCR/text-extraction tools get a flat, rectified image.

It is backed by the UVDoc neural grid-based document unwarping model.

Available Crunchers

dewarp

Dewarps a single document image, straightening out perspective and curl distortion.

  • Why this is important: OCR and layout-analysis tools generally assume a flat, top-down view of the page. Photos taken at an angle or of a curved page (e.g. a book) reduce recognition accuracy; running dewarp first improves results.
