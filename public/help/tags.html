Tags

Classification of material is fundamental part of archive based research.

It can happen in multiple levels starting from rought usable/non-usable classification.

Tags in MessyDesk

Tags are not just labels in MessyDesk but they are full-blown database objects that can have their own metadata.

You can browse your data with your tags.

You can create new sets based on tags with Set filters.
